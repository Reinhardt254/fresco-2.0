Reliable Quality, Consistent Supply

Our vertically integrated system minimizes supply disruptions and ensures dependable availability. Fresco Flavors delivers premium organic herbs with consistent quality, helping retailers maintain reliable shelf presence and meet customer demand.

Controlled Sourcing: Seed To Jar Oversight

Fresco Flavors implements a controlled sourcing model that oversees the process from farmer selection, cultivation to harvesting to the point the herbs reach the freeze-drying facility. This is to ensure consistent quality standards and dependable supply for our retail partners.

Food Safety & Full Traceability

Our operations follow internationally recognized food safety standards and meet U.S. regulatory expectations, including FDA guidelines. Every pack is batch-coded and fully traceable from harvest date to processing providing accountability and retailer confidence.
