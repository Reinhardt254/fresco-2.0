Instructions

Experience the true essence of summer all year round with Fresco Flavors Freeze-Dried Basil. Combined with the savory depth of Fresco Flavors Thyme and Garlic, this soup provides a high-antioxidant, nutrient-dense meal that balances rich tomato acidity with the unmistakable, "just-picked" sweetness of premium basil.

  1. 1

    Sauté: Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and sauté for 5 minutes until translucent. Stir in the garlic, thyme, and oregano, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.

  2. 2

    Simmer: Add the tomatoes and their juices, the broth, and the sugar. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes to allow the herbs to infuse the tomato base.

  3. 3

    Blend: Use an immersion blender to puree the soup until smooth. If using a standard blender, work in batches and be careful with the hot liquid.

  4. 4

    Finish: Stir in the cream (if using) and season with salt and pepper.

  5. 5

    The Herb Burst: Just before serving, stir in 1/4 cup basil. The heat will wilt the basil slightly, releasing its essential oils.

  6. 6

    Serve: Ladle into bowls and garnish with the remaining 1/4 basil for a bright, aromatic finish.

Ingredients

Servings:
5
  • Ripe tomatoes, halved (or crushed tomatoes) 1.5 kg (or 2 cans)
  • Fresco Flavors Freeze-dried Garlic 4 cloves
  • Fresco Flavors Freeze-dried Basil 1/2 cup
  • Fresco Flavors Freeze-dried Thyme 1 tsp
  • Fresco Flavors Freeze-dried Oregano 1 tsp
  • Onion, finely chopped 1 medium
  • Vegetable or chicken broth 500 ml
  • Heavy cream or coconut milk (optional) 1/2 cup
  • Olive oil 2 tbsp
  • Sugar (to balance acidity) 1 tsp
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
