Elevated Avocado Toast & Eggs

Prep Time: 10m | Cook Time: 5m | Serves: 5

KEY PRODUCTS

freeze-dried Cilantro
$9.00
freeze-dried Scallions
$9.00

Instructions

Transform a simple breakfast into a gourmet experience using the "instant-fresh" taste of Fresco Flavors. This simple everyday breakfast recipe using Fresco Flavors Chives and Cilantro sprinkled over creamy avocado and warm eggs, these herbs rehydrate on contact, providing a burst of organic nutrients and a professional-grade finish to your morning routine.

  1. 1

    The Mash: In a small bowl, mash the avocados with lemon juice, salt, pepper, and Fresco Flavors Scallions. The scallions will add a subtle, savory crunch to the creamy base.

  2. 2

    The Toast: Spread the avocado mixture generously over each slice of warm toast.

  3. 3

    The Eggs: Top each toast with a freshly cooked egg.

  4. 4

    The Garnish: Immediately sprinkle the top with Fresco Flavors Chives and Cilantro. The steam from the egg will activate the herbs, releasing their fresh aroma.

  5. 5

    Serve: Finish with a pinch of red pepper flakes if desired.

Ingredients

Servings:
5
  • Sourdough or whole-grain bread, toasted 5 slices
  • Avocados 3 ripe
  • Eggs (poached or fried) 5 large
  • Lemon juice 1 tbsp
  • Fresco Flavors freeze-dried Chives 1 tbsp
  • Fresco Flavors freeze-dried Cilantro 1 tbsp
  • Fresco Flavors freeze-dried Scallions 1 tsp
  • Red pepper flakes (optional) to taste
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
