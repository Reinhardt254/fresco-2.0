Slow-Simmered Herbed Beef & Veggie Stew

Prep Time: 20m | Cook Time: 1h 45m | Serves: 5

Instructions

There is nothing quite like the cozy aroma of a stew bubbling away on the stove. This recipe relies on Fresco Flavors Herbs to bring that "plucked-from-the-garden" magic straight to your kitchen. As they simmer in the savory beef broth, the herbs gently awaken, infusing every bite with a deep, soulful flavor that feels like a warm hug. It’s a simple, hearty way to turn a few fresh ingredients into a truly comforting home-cooked masterpiece.

  1. 1

    Coat & Brown: Toss beef cubes with flour, salt, and pepper. Heat oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot. Brown the beef in batches; remove and set aside.

  2. 2

    Aromatics: In the same pot, sauté onions and garlic for 3 minutes. Stir in the rosemary, thyme, and oregano.

  3. 3

    Simmer: Return beef to the pot and add the broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 1 hour.

  4. 4

    Vegetables: Stir in carrots and potatoes. Cover and simmer for another 30–40 minutes until the meat and vegetables are fork-tender.

  5. 5

    Serve: Serve warm with crusty bread or rice.

Ingredients

Servings:
5
  • Beef stew meat, cut into 1-inch cubes 750 g
  • Flour 2 tbsp
  • Oil 2 tbsp
  • Fresco Flavors freeze-dried Garlic 2 tbs
  • Fresco Flavors freeze-dried Rosemary 1 tsp
  • Fresco Flavors freeze-dried Thyme 1 tsp
  • Fresco Flavors freeze-dried Oregano 1 tsp
  • Carrots, sliced into rounds 3
  • Potatoes, peeled and cubed 4 medium
  • Onion, chopped 1 large
  • Beef broth or water 500 ml
  • Salt and pepper to taste
