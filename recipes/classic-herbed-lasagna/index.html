Classic Herbed Lasagna

Prep Time: 30m | Cook Time: 45m | Serves: 6-8

Instructions

A great lasagna depends on a sauce with deep, layered aromatics. Fresco Flavors Freeze-Dried Basil and Oregano for a superior alternative to traditional dried herbs, offering the sweet, peppery notes of fresh Italian herbs that permeate every layer of cheese and pasta. By using freeze-dried herbs in the ricotta mixture, you ensure the nutritional integrity and vibrant flavor profile remain intact throughout the baking process.

  1. 1

    The Meat Sauce: Brown the beef in a pan. Drain fat. Stir in Fresco Flavors Garlic, Basil, and Oregano. Add marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes to allow the freeze-dried herbs to fully rehydrate and flavor the sauce.

  2. 2

    The Cheese Mix: In a medium bowl, combine ricotta, egg, parmesan, and Fresco Flavors Parsley.

  3. 3

    Layer: In a 9x13 inch baking dish, layer sauce, noodles, then the ricotta mixture and mozzarella. Repeat layers, ending with mozzarella on top.

  4. 4

    Bake: Cover with foil and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes until bubbly and golden.

  5. 5

    Rest: Let stand for 10 minutes before slicing to allow the herb-infused layers to set.

Ingredients

Servings:
6
  • Ground beef or Italian sausage 500 g
  • Marinara sauce 1 jar (700 ml)
  • Fresco Flavors Freeze-dried Garlic 2 tbsp
  • Fresco Flavors Freeze-dried Basil 1 tbsp
  • Fresco Flavors Freeze-dried Oregano 1 tsp
  • Ricotta cheese 500 g
  • Egg, lightly beaten 1
  • Mozzarella cheese, shredded 300 g
  • Parmesan cheese 1/2 cup
  • Lasagna noodles, cooked 12
  • Fresco Flavors Freeze-dried Parsley (for ricotta mix) 1 tbsp
