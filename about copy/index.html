About Us

Fresco Flavors is a premium brand of herbs and spices, 100% organic.

OUR STORY

Fresco Flavors delivers premium, organic herbs grown in Kenya’s ideal agricultural climate — rich volcanic soils, high-altitude sunshine, and year-round growing conditions that naturally enhance flavor and potency. Our herbs are cultivated under certified organic standards, without synthetic pesticides or preservatives, ensuring purity at every stage.

We operate a single-source supply model, managing the full value chain from seedling propagation and cultivation to harvesting, freeze-drying, and packaging. This vertical integration guarantees consistency, food safety compliance, and complete traceability. Every pack is batch-coded and traceable back to origin, reinforcing transparency and retailer confidence.

OUR MISSION

Our success is inseparable from the communities we work with. Through our partnership with Freezedry Harvest Kenya, we create sustainable employment and uphold fair-trade practices that strengthen entire households. Every pack of herbs represents real impact farmers earning dignified incomes and operations aligned with global ESG and sustainability standards. We don’t just export herbs; we export opportunity, resilience, and shared prosperity.

OUR COMMITMENT

Our commitment to you is to provide the best herbs and spices in the world.

Quality & Natural Integrity

Our herbs are organically grown and carefully freeze-dried to preserve their natural qualities without artificial additives, delivering pure flavor and dependable quality in every pack.

Commitment to Transparency

We believe customers deserve to know exactly what goes into their food. That’s why we provide clear ingredient sourcing and production information, ensuring full confidence in every product you choose.

Social & Environmental Impact

We support sustainable agriculture and responsible production practices while creating meaningful employment opportunities for local communities across Kenya, from our organic farms to our processing facilities.
