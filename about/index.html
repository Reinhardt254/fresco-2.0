OUR STORY

Fresco Flavors delivers premium, organic herbs grown in Kenya’s ideal agricultural climate rich volcanic soils, high-altitude sunshine, and year-round growing conditions that naturally enhance flavor and potency. Our herbs are cultivated under certified organic standards, without synthetic pesticides or preservatives, ensuring purity at every stage.

We work directly at origin, managing the full value chain from cultivation to harvesting, freeze-drying, and packaging. This vertical integration guarantees consistency, food safety compliance, and complete traceability. Every pack is batch-coded and traceable back to origin, reinforcing transparency and retailer confidence. Fresco isn’t just organic, it’s origin-controlled, supply-secure and shelf-ready.
