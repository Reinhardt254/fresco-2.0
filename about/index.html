OUR STORY

In a global supply chain often defined by blended origins and fragmented processing, we choose a different path. We operate close to the source, working within a tightly controlled agricultural ecosystem where cultivation, harvesting, and freeze-drying are strategically aligned.

Fresco Flavors operates in Kenya’s equatorial growing belt, one of the few regions globally that allows continuous, year-round organic herb cultivation. This consistency eliminates seasonal gaps and reduces the volatility often associated with multi-origin sourcing. By harvesting at peak vitality and freeze-drying immediately at our on-site facility, we capture herbs at their freshest moment preserving flavor, nutrients, and integrity before degradation begins.

Fresco Flavors was designed with modern retail in mind. Our systems prioritize traceability, clean-label integrity, and predictable supply ensuring consistent SKU performance across regional and national distribution.

OUR PROCESS

1

Planting

Seedlings are grown and distributed to vetted farmers, ensuring rigorous quality control from the very start.

2

Harvesting

Herbs are harvested at peak maturity from our controlled organic farms, maximizing natural potency and flavor.

3

Processing

Herbs are flash freeze-dried at our facility, perfectly preserving retail-grade flavor, aroma, and vibrant structure.

4

Packaging

Traceable, batch-coded packaging ensures product integrity and full safety compliance for your retail shelves.

5

Distribution

Streamlined logistics deliver export-ready herbs directly to your distribution centers within guaranteed timelines.
